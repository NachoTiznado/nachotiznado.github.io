Featured research

A selection of publications spanning accessibility, transport equity, mobility, and justice.

Public transport accessibility accounting for level of service and competition for urban opportunities

Journal of Transport Geography · 2021

How should we measure whether public transport provides equitable access to opportunities? This paper develops an accessibility measure that accounts for both transit level of service and competition for opportunities, and applies it to education in Santiago de Chile.

accessibility · transport equity · public transport · Santiago

Transport and equity in Latin America: a critical review of socially oriented accessibility assessments

Transport Reviews · 2020

This review examines how transportation research in Latin America has approached equity through accessibility assessment. It identifies key conceptual and methodological gaps and discusses how accessibility measures can better account for social inequalities and the experiences of different population groups.

transport equity · accessibility · Latin America · transport justice

Exploring the interplay of transportation, time poverty, and activity participation

Transportation Research Interdisciplinary Perspectives · 2024

Time is a fundamental resource for participating in everyday life, yet transportation can consume a substantial share of it. This paper examines the relationship between transport disadvantage, time poverty, and activity participation, highlighting how excessive travel time can constrain people's ability to engage in activities and affect wellbeing.

time poverty · transport disadvantage · activity participation · wellbeing

Tracking cycling behaviour and comfort perceptions in Toronto: Trends from 1999 to 2023

Transportation Research Part A · 2026

How have cycling patterns and perceptions of comfort changed as Toronto's cycling infrastructure has evolved? This paper examines long-term trends in cycling behaviour and comfort perceptions, highlighting the relationship between the urban cycling environment and people's experiences of cycling.

cycling · cycling infrastructure · comfort · Toronto

Profiling caregivers: caregiving workload, mobility, stress, and remote work difficulties

Urban Studies · 2025

Caregiving responsibilities shape how people use their time, travel, work, and experience everyday mobility. This paper identifies distinct caregiver profiles based on caregiving workload, mobility, stress, and difficulties with remote work, revealing important differences in the lived experience of mobility and care.

caregiving · mobility of care · stress · remote work

Standards of fairness in the transportation literature

Transportation · 2025

What does it mean for a transportation policy or intervention to be fair? This paper examines how fairness, equity, and standards are conceptualized in the transportation literature and proposes a framework for making the normative assumptions behind transportation research more explicit.

transport justice · fairness · equity · accessibility
